# Resolve Strategy Style Guide

Visual mood: Assured, analytical, modern, and quietly optimistic

Color palette:

- Primary: `#1B3A5C` | Accent: `#E8A020` | Background: `#F5F6F8` | Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- On-primary: `#FFFFFF` | On-surface: `#1A1F2E` | Muted: `#5A6478` | Border: `#D9DDE4`
- GradientStart: `#1B3A5C` | GradientEnd: `#356A89`
- Success: `#287D4A` | Warning: `#B35C1E`
- ChipBackground: `#FFF2DA` | ChipContent: `#7A4D00`

Corner radius: 12dp | Elevation: restrained tonal elevation | Spacing: comfortable 8dp grid

Typography: Headline=bold, Body=regular, Accent=uppercase with tracked lettering

Font pair: Heading=Syne, Body=DM Sans

Buttons: solid primary, full-width for key actions | Cards: clean surfaces with clear hierarchy | Icons: outlined and purposeful

Images: documentary business photography, cropped confidently with subtle rounding

Feel: The interface translates the website’s structured grid and amber highlights into a clear consulting experience. It feels credible and decisive without becoming corporate or cold.

Layout personality: functional
